# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Positive behavior:** empathy, respectful feedback, accepting responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

**Unacceptable behavior:** harassment, trolling, insults, sexual language or imagery, publishing others’ private information, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may ban contributors temporarily or permanently.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/scrollDynasty/react-native-drum-picker/issues) (for public matters) or a **private security advisory** for sensitive reports.
